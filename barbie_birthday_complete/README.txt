BARBIE BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

1. Keep index.html and the photos folder together.
2. Open index.html in Chrome to preview the website.
3. Your first two pictures are already installed as photos/photo1.jpg and photos/photo2.jpg.
4. To add the remaining two later, place your images in the photos folder and name them photo3.jpg and photo4.jpg.
5. Upload index.html and the entire photos folder together to your website host (for example, GitHub Pages). Do not upload index.html by itself.

You can edit the captions and birthday message in index.html using Notepad.
